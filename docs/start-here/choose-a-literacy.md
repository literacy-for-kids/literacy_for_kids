# Choose a Literacy

You do not need to choose perfectly. Pick the literacy that matches the child's question, current friction point, or group goal. You can always add a Shared Toolkit when a skill need appears — and you can always switch.

## Start with the question

| If the child is asking… | Start with… | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "How do computers, files, coding, AI, or online tools work?" | [Computer Literacy](https://computer.literacy-for-kids.com/) | Technology as a system kids can understand and use responsibly |
| "How do money, saving, spending, wants, needs, or tradeoffs work?" | [Financial Literacy](https://financial.literacy-for-kids.com/) | Money as a system of value, planning, and tradeoffs |
| "How do I make better choices?" | [Decision Literacy](https://decision.literacy-for-kids.com/) | Clear thinking, luck vs. skill, and weighing options |
| "How do ads, news, social media, sources, and claims work?" | [Media Literacy](https://media.literacy-for-kids.com/) | How information is constructed, spread, and checked |
| "How do communities, rules, government, voting, and public decisions work?" | [Civic Literacy](https://civic.literacy-for-kids.com/) | How groups organize, decide, and solve shared problems |
| "How do rules, rights, responsibilities, agreements, and fairness work?" | [Legal Literacy](https://legal.literacy-for-kids.com/) | The operating rules of society, at kid scale |
| "How do feelings, friendships, boundaries, trust, repair, and group dynamics work?" | [Emotional & Social Literacy](https://emotional.literacy-for-kids.com/) | Internal signals and social systems |
| "How do ecosystems, waste, water, climate, resources, and big environmental loops work?" | [Environmental Systems Literacy](https://environmental.literacy-for-kids.com/) | Earth as a physical system with loops and limits |
| "How do body systems, habits, sleep, stress, movement, food, and trusted health questions work?" | [Health Systems Literacy](https://health.literacy-for-kids.com/) | The body as a system worth understanding and maintaining |

## Start with the friction point

| If the friction is… | Try this literacy | Add this Shared Toolkit |
|---|---|---|
| overwhelmed by a digital project | Computer Literacy | [Executive Function](../toolkits/executive-function/intro.md) or [Information Organization](../toolkits/information-organization/intro.md) |
| confused by online claims | Media Literacy | [Information Organization](../toolkits/information-organization/intro.md) |
| arguing about a group project | Civic, Computer, Media, or Environmental | [Collaboration](../toolkits/collaboration/intro.md) |
| worried about money choices | Financial Literacy | Decision Literacy or [Information Organization](../toolkits/information-organization/intro.md) |
| friendship conflict | Emotional & Social Literacy | [Communication](../toolkits/communication/intro.md) or [Coping](../toolkits/coping-skills/intro.md) |
| rules feel unfair or confusing | Civic or Legal Literacy | [Communication](../toolkits/communication/intro.md) or [Problem Solving](../toolkits/problem-solving/intro.md) |
| climate or waste worry | Environmental Systems Literacy | [Coping](../toolkits/coping-skills/intro.md) or [Problem Solving](../toolkits/problem-solving/intro.md) |
| health routine confusion | Health Systems Literacy | [Executive Function](../toolkits/executive-function/intro.md) |

## If you still are not sure

- Start with the child's own question.
- Choose the topic that has energy.
- Use one lesson, not a whole curriculum.
- If the issue is more skill than topic, start with a [Shared Toolkit](./use-shared-toolkits.md).
- If the issue is unsafe, medical, legal, therapeutic, or crisis-related, get appropriate adult or professional support first.

For a deeper comparison of the nine curricula — including settings, styles, and who each fits best — see the [Curriculum Adoption Checklist](../curriculum-adoption-checklist.md).

## Good pairings

- Computer + Information Organization
- Media + Information Organization
- Civic + Collaboration
- Environmental + Collaboration
- Financial + Decision
- Emotional & Social + Communication or Coping
- Health + Executive Function
- Legal + Information Organization
