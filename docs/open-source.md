# Open Source

All Literacy for Kids curricula are open source, free to use, and designed to be adapted.

## What that means

- **Free** — every curriculum is available at no cost
- **Open** — all content is publicly available and transparently developed
- **Adaptable** — educators are encouraged to modify lessons, reorder content, and adjust materials to fit their learners
- **Community-driven** — contributions, suggestions, and feedback are welcome

## How to adapt

You can use the curricula exactly as they are, or you can:

- rearrange lessons to match your schedule
- skip lessons that do not fit your learners
- add your own examples, discussions, or activities
- translate materials into another language
- fork a repository and build your own version

The materials are designed to be a starting point. Make them your own.
