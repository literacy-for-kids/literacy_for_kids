# 📰 Media Literacy

Media Literacy teaches students how to evaluate information, recognize bias, and understand algorithms.

## Why it matters

Children are surrounded by messages, headlines, videos, and recommendations. They need tools for sorting reliable information from confusion, persuasion, or noise.

## What students learn

- how to evaluate sources and claims
- how bias and framing affect understanding
- how algorithms influence what people see online
- how to pause before sharing or trusting information
