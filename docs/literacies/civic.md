# 🏛 Civic Literacy

Civic Literacy teaches students how societies organize themselves and how citizens shape their communities.

## Why it matters

Children live within families, schools, neighborhoods, and larger civic systems. Understanding rules, institutions, and participation helps them see how communities work and how they can contribute.

## What students learn

- how communities make and follow shared rules
- how institutions help organize public life
- how citizens participate in civic life
- how local choices can shape a community
