# 🧠 Decision Literacy

Decision Literacy teaches students how to think clearly, evaluate choices, and understand consequences.

## Why it matters

Children make decisions every day. Learning to pause, compare options, and think about outcomes helps them in school, relationships, and daily life.

## What students learn

- how to break a choice into smaller parts
- how to notice trade-offs and possible consequences
- how to ask better questions before acting
- how to reflect on decisions after the fact
