# Printable Learning Cards

Simple cards you can copy, print, or rewrite by hand. Keep them nearby — a step you can actually find in the moment beats a beautiful chart you can't. Cut them out, tape them inside a notebook, or keep them on a shelf.

:::caution Everyday learning skills, not a diagnosis
These cards teach everyday learning skills. They are not therapy, medical advice, disability evaluation, or a replacement for trusted adults, educators, caregivers, or qualified support. Kids should not be treated as lazy or broken when learning is hard. If learning feels consistently inaccessible, unsafe, or overwhelming, asking a trusted adult for support is part of learning.
:::

---

## Learning Loop Card

> **THE LEARNING LOOP**
>
> 1. What am I trying to learn?
> 2. What part do I understand?
> 3. What part is confusing?
> 4. How can I practice remembering?
> 5. What mistake taught me something?
> 6. What feedback can help?
> 7. When will I practice again?

---

## Quick Learning Loop Card

> **WHEN LEARNING FEELS HARD**
>
> - Name the skill.
> - Notice what you know.
> - Find the confusing part.
> - Practice remembering.
> - Use mistakes as clues.
> - Get feedback.
> - Practice again later.

---

## Forgetting Check Card

> **FORGETTING IS NORMAL**
>
> - What did I remember?
> - What was fuzzy?
> - What do I need to practice again?
> - When will I come back to it?

---

## Look / Cover / Try / Check Card

> **PRACTICE REMEMBERING**
>
> - Look at the idea.
> - Cover it.
> - Try to say, write, draw, or use it.
> - Check what was right.
> - Practice the fuzzy part again.

---

## Mistake Data Card

> **MISTAKES ARE DATA**
>
> - Mistake: ___
> - What it shows: ___
> - Next practice: ___
> - Who or what can help: ___

## Teach Back Card

> **TEACH IT TO LEARN IT**
>
> - Big idea: ___
> - Example: ___

> - Question I still have: ___

## Better Learning Question Card

> **ASK A CLEARER QUESTION**
>
> - I am trying to learn ___.
> - I understand ___.
> - I am stuck on ___.
> - I tried ___.
> - Can you help me with ___?

> - Fuzzy part: ___
> - Practice method: ___
> - Time: ___
> - Next practice: ___
> - How I will check: ___
> - Who can help: ___

---

## Ask for Support Card

> **SOME LEARNING NEEDS A TRUSTED ADULT**
>
> Ask a trusted adult for support if:
>
> - learning feels overwhelming all the time
> - you feel unsafe
> - you feel ashamed or scared to ask
> - you cannot access what you need
> - the problem feels bigger than everyday practice
>
> *Asking for support can be part of learning.*

---

Learning tools help with everyday practice. They do not replace trusted adults, educators, caregivers, or qualified support when learning feels consistently inaccessible, unsafe, or overwhelming.
