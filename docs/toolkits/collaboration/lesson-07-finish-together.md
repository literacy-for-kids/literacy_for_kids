# Finish Together

**Short summary:** Finishing together includes checking done, giving credit, cleaning up, and reflecting on how the group worked.

## Big idea

Finishing is more than turning something in. A group finishes together when it checks the shared goal, credits who contributed what, cleans up, and notices what to do differently next time.

## Why it matters

Credit matters because invisible work breeds resentment — the planner, the checker, and the encourager did real work too. Cleanup matters because someone always gets stuck with it if the group does not share it. And reflection matters because the next group project starts wherever this one ended.

## Kid-friendly explanation

A group project is not finished just because it is *almost* there. A pilot does not stop flying when the airport is in sight — the group still has to land the plane: check done, give credit, clean up, and reflect.

## Tool: the Finish Together Check

> - Did we meet the shared goal?
> - What still needs to be checked?
> - Who contributed what?
> - What needs cleanup or handoff?
> - What worked well?
> - What should change next time?

## Activity: Almost Done or Finished?

Sort each situation — almost done, or actually finished?

- Slides are done but nobody knows who presents.
- The model is built but materials are everywhere.
- The paper is written but sources are missing.
- The poster is complete and everyone's role is credited.
- The group turned it in but one person was left out of the credits.

Discuss what "finishing together" would require in each case.

## Discussion questions

- Why does credit matter?
- What does cleanup have to do with collaboration?
- How can a group finish without blaming?
- What should a group remember for next time?

## Try it this week

At the end of one shared task, ask: "What worked, who helped, and what should change next time?"

## Adult note

Help kids see invisible contributions. Planning, organizing, encouraging, checking, and repairing can all be real work — a finish-together reflection is where that work finally becomes visible.
