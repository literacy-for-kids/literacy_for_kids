# The Five Literacies Framework

The Literacy for Kids framework gives children a practical mental model of how the modern world works.

Instead of focusing on one subject alone, the framework introduces five connected ways of understanding daily life.

## A simple progression

### 1. Decision Literacy

Decision Literacy is the foundation for thinking and reasoning. It helps students slow down, evaluate choices, and notice consequences.

### 2. Computer Literacy

Computer Literacy helps students understand the machines they interact with every day and how to use them responsibly.

### 3. Media Literacy

Media Literacy helps students understand information systems, evaluate claims, recognize bias, and notice how algorithms shape what they see.

### 4. Financial Literacy

Financial Literacy helps students understand value, trade, resources, and how present choices can affect future options.

### 5. Civic Literacy

Civic Literacy helps students understand social systems, shared rules, and how people shape their communities and governments.

## How the literacies connect

These literacies reinforce each other:

- better decision-making supports every other area
- computer literacy helps students understand the tools behind media
- media literacy supports better choices and stronger civic participation
- financial literacy applies reasoning to resources and trade-offs
- civic literacy brings together ideas about systems, rules, and shared responsibility

They are designed to work well together, but each curriculum can also be taught on its own.

If you want a full sequence, you can follow the progression above. If you need a single starting point, choose the literacy that best matches your learners and setting.
